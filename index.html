Learn • Reading

Food Detectives 📖

Read this story carefully. Look for details about food and drinks. You can listen to the story too!

On Saturday, Aina and Ben are planning a picnic at the park. Aina opens the cupboard. There are some biscuits and a loaf of bread. Ben looks in the fridge. There is some milk and a carton of orange juice, but there are no apples. Aina says, “Let's buy some apples, grapes and sandwiches.” Ben adds water to their shopping list. They are excited for their picnic!
🔎 Reading tip: Find the words in the question, then look for the same information in the story.
